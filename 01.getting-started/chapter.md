# Getting Started with Documents Seller

Documents Seller adds a digital downloads store to your Joomla site. You can sell ebooks, templates, software, and other downloadable files, or offer them for free. Customers browse your catalog through Joomla menu items, place an order for paid products, and receive access to their files after payment is confirmed.

## How the store works

- **Files and documents.** A file is what the customer downloads. A document is the product listing that sets the title, price, file, and download limits. To deliver several files as one product, upload them as a single ZIP archive.
- **Categories and menus.** Categories organize your documents. Joomla menu items give customers a way to browse categories, open a product, or return to their purchases.
- **Payments and delivery.** An online payment plugin can confirm payment automatically. With Offline Payment, you approve the order after you receive the money. Customers can get their files through email links or attachments, and signed-in customers can return to eligible purchases through **Orders History**.
- **Free downloads.** You can give visitors direct access to a free file or ask them to fill in a form first.

## Set up your store

- [Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start) walks you through publishing one paid product and testing checkout, order approval, and the download.
- [Installation](/getting-started/installation) covers the Joomla requirements, the extension package, and how to install and update it.
- [Configuration](/getting-started/configuration) explains the settings for checkout, accounts, file delivery, taxes, invoices, and storefront display.

Once your first purchase works, use [Setup](/setup) to expand the catalog and customize checkout. Use [Storefront Menus and Modules](/frontend-menu-options) to plan how customers find products and retrieve purchases. After launch, use [Orders Management](/orders-management) to handle customer orders and [Dashboard and Sales Charts](/orders-management/dashboard-and-sales-charts) to monitor sales.

To add customers to mailing lists after a purchase, see [Newsletter Integrations](/newsletter-integrations) for AcyMailing and Mailchimp setup guides.
