Choose a screen based on what you want to check in the Joomla administrator.

| Screen | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| [Revenue Chart](/orders-management/revenue-chart) | Analyze revenue or order counts over a selected month range, compare with the previous year, and compare selected documents on separate lines. |
| **Documents Seller → Dashboard** | Check store totals, recent orders, and quick sales trends. |
| **Documents Seller → Sales Chart** | Track daily or monthly sales for the whole store or for orders containing a selected document. |
| [Sales Report](/orders-management#sales-report) | Compare products by item sales, income, and new customers over a selected period. |
| [Orders → Export](/orders-management#export-orders) | Download individual order records as a CSV file. |

## Dashboard

Open **Documents Seller → Dashboard**. The period selector above the summary cards changes **Total Earnings**, **Orders**, and **New Customers**. **Total Earnings** sums paid orders. **Orders** and **New Customers** can also include pending offline orders. The **Documents** card counts published products.

Use the seven-day and 13-month sales charts for recent trends. The dashboard also shows sales by country for the past year, recent orders, and lists of best-selling, latest, and most popular documents. The **Most Popular Documents** list uses document hit counts.

## Sales Chart

1. Open **Documents Seller → Sales Chart**.
2. Leave the product selector set to **All** for store-wide sales or select a document to include only orders containing it.
3. Use **Last 30 days Sales Chart** to review daily income. Use **Last 13 Months Sales Chart** to review monthly income through the current month.

The charts use the order creation date and sum the recorded totals of paid orders. When you select a document, a cart order containing that document contributes its full order total. Use the [Sales Report](/orders-management#sales-report) for income based on document line items.
