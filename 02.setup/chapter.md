Use these guides to build your catalog, choose how customers pay, and customize checkout and order emails. If you are setting up Documents Seller for the first time, follow [Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start) to test a purchase from checkout to download.

## Build the catalog

- [Upload Files](/setup/upload-files) explains how to upload the files customers will receive, including large files you transfer directly to the server.
- [Categories](/setup/categories) explains how to organize documents into categories and subcategories.
- [Documents](/setup/documents) covers pricing, product descriptions, tags, and download limits.

## Configure checkout and delivery

- [Custom Fields](/setup/custom-fields) shows how to collect customer information or add fees based on checkout selections.
- [Automatic Checkout Field Population](/setup/custom-fields/automatic-checkout-field-population) shows how to reuse signed-in customers' Joomla or Community Builder profile details at checkout.
- [Payment Plugins](/setup/payment-plugins) explains how to select, configure, and publish payment methods.
- [Coupons](/setup/coupons) covers discount codes and when the coupon field appears at checkout.
- [Emails & Messages](/setup/emails-messages) explains when each template is used and how to include order details, payment instructions, or download links.
- [Translation](/setup/translation) explains how to edit interface text and translate Documents Seller into other languages.
- [Enable Captcha](/setup/enable-captcha) shows how to add your Joomla CAPTCHA to the order form.

When your catalog is ready, use [Storefront Menus and Modules](/frontend-menu-options) to give customers a way to browse it.
