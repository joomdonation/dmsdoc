Use these plugins to display documents inside Joomla articles, subscribe customers to AcyMailing lists, or include documents in your site's Smart Search results. The plugins come with the Documents Seller package. Open **System → Manage → Plugins** in the Joomla administrator to enable the ones you want to use.

| Plugin | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| [Content - DMS Category](/plugins/content-dms-category) | Display a category's documents inside a Joomla article, such as a product guide or landing page. |
| [Documents Seller - AcyMailing](/plugins/acymailing) | Add customers to site-wide or document-specific mailing lists when their orders are confirmed. |
| [Smart Search - Documents Seller](/plugins/smart-search) | Let visitors find documents alongside other site content in Joomla Smart Search. |
